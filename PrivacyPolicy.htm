1. Introduction

Welcome to MindBridge ("we," "our," or "us").

MindBridge is an AI-powered emotional support platform designed to help teenagers navigate everyday emotional challenges, including exam anxiety, self-doubt, academic pressure, and personal growth. Our approach is inspired by cognitive behavioral therapy (CBT), emotional validation, and growth mindset principles.

We understand that conversations about emotions and mental well-being can be deeply personal. We are committed to treating your information with care, respect, and transparency.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we may collect, how we use it, when it may be shared, how we protect it, and what choices you have regarding your information when using MindBridge.

By using MindBridge, you acknowledge that you have read this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

The information we collect depends on how you use MindBridge and which features you access.

2.1 Information You Provide

Depending on the features available, we may collect:

  • Account information: Such as your name, email address, username, or other registration details.
  • Conversation content: Messages, questions, and responses exchanged with the AI assistant.
  • Emotional well-being information: Information you voluntarily share about your feelings, stress, exam anxiety, self-doubt, academic experiences, or personal challenges.
  • Feedback and support requests: Information you provide when reporting a problem, submitting feedback, or contacting us.

Please avoid sharing unnecessary identifying information, passwords, financial information, or other highly sensitive personal details in conversations.

2.2 Information Collected Automatically

Depending on our app configuration and the services we use, we may collect:

  • Device type, operating system, and app version.
  • App usage information and interaction events.
  • Diagnostic information, crash reports, and performance data.
  • IP address and technical identifiers, where collected by our systems or service providers.

We will disclose the specific categories collected by the released version of MindBridge and update this policy when our practices change.

2.3 Information We Do Not Intentionally Collect

MindBridge does not intentionally request information unrelated to providing its features. Any additional collection, including location information, contacts, microphone access, or other device permissions, should be disclosed here if the app uses those capabilities.

3. How We Use Your Information

Subject to our actual data practices, we may use collected information to:

  • Provide AI-powered emotional support and respond to your messages.
  • Offer reflections and exercises inspired by CBT and growth mindset principles.
  • Help you explore everyday challenges, such as exam anxiety and academic stress.
  • Maintain and manage your account.
  • Remember conversation context or preferences when the relevant features are enabled.
  • Monitor app performance, troubleshoot errors, and improve reliability.
  • Respond to support requests and communicate important service updates.
  • Detect abuse, protect platform security, and comply with applicable legal obligations.

We will not use personal information for purposes incompatible with this policy without providing appropriate notice or obtaining consent where required.

4. AI-Powered Conversations

MindBridge uses artificial intelligence to generate responses to user messages.

How AI processes your messages

When you interact with the AI assistant, your messages will not be processed by MindBridge. As we appreciate the privacy and because users may discuss personal emotions and difficult experiences, all the conversation are limited to the user only.

MindBridge has feature for the users to integrate their own AI engine API key for this purpose.

AI responses may be inaccurate, incomplete, or inappropriate. They should not be treated as a professional diagnosis or as a replacement for care from a qualified mental health professional.

Use of conversations for AI training

We do not store any conversations or any data related to the AI chats or messages.

5. Emotional Well-Being and Sensitive Information

MindBridge is designed to support everyday emotional reflection and personal growth. Users may voluntarily share information about their feelings, stress, or personal circumstances.

We use such information only for disclosed purposes consistent with the app's functionality and applicable law.

We do not claim to provide medical diagnosis or treatment through the AI assistant. Any features that assess mental health, create emotional profiles, or infer sensitive characteristics must be accurately described here before launch.

We encourage users to seek help from a trusted adult or qualified professional when they need support beyond what an AI application can provide.

6. Teenagers and Children's Privacy

MindBridge is designed specifically for teenagers. We recognize that younger users deserve additional privacy protections.

We will handle young users' information in accordance with applicable privacy, child-protection, and data-protection laws.

Where required, we will implement appropriate age-related notices, parental consent mechanisms, and other safeguards.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in circumstances where such collection is prohibited or where legally required consent has not been obtained.

Parents or legal guardians who believe a child has provided personal information inappropriately may contact us using the details below.
